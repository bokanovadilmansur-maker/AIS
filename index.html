About us

National values and global citizenship

Our mission

Building a prosperous society by preserving national values and nurturing global citizenship.

Aqbobek International School grew out of the Aqbobek school, founded in 2012, and became an international school in 2020. Our modern campus is equipped with technology and resources for a comfortable, inspiring learning environment.

  • CISAccredited to Council of International Schools standards
  • MCEMarshall Cavendish Education curriculum, Singapore
  • MoE RKState licence and full accreditation
  • IELTSOfficial IELTS test centre
Programmes

From pre-school to university

The child is the main participant in learning, and their interests and opinions matter to us. Every child has the right to be seen, heard and understood.

Grade 0

Pre-school

  • Singapore curriculum taught in English
  • Small groups of 8–15 children
  • Afternoon nap and clubs after lunch
Grades 1–4

Primary school

  • Fully aligned with the state education standard
  • Singapore method: maths, English, science
  • Literacy, reading speed and working with texts
Grades 5–9

Middle school

  • Extra hours of maths and English
  • Olympiad preparation and project-based learning
  • English-medium classes and equipped labs
Grades 10–11

High school

  • UNT preparation with regular mock exams
  • IELTS, SAT and NUET with top specialists
  • Career guidance and an individual learning path
